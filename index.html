Ask us anything ↗
Do I need to understand IT or AI?

No. Tell us which tasks are taking too much time. We explain the options in plain English and guide you through the process.

Can I keep using my existing software?

Often, yes. We start by understanding the tools you already use and check what can be connected before proposing any change.

Is automation expensive?

It depends on the task, the tools involved and the level of support you need. We can start with one manageable workflow and agree on scope before work begins.

Will I still be in control?

Yes. We design workflows with appropriate approvals and human review, especially where money, personal information or important decisions are involved.
